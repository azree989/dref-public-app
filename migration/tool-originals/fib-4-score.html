FIB-4 Score Calculator

This tool calculates BMI and Fibrosis-4 Index (FIB-4). Under POA NCD 2026, FIB-4 scoring should proceed for patients with BMI ≥27.5 kg/m².

FIB-4 Formula
FIB-4 = Age × AST / [ Platelet Count × √ALT ]
Age in years, AST in U/L, ALT in U/L, Platelet Count in 10⁹/L
