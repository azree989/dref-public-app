Strep Pharyngitis Score Calculator

Clinical Pathways for Primary Care: Acute Pharyngitis

Recommended Management

Score < 3: Symptomatic treatment (e.g., antipyretic, analgesic, hydration).

Recommended Management

Score ≥ 3: Symptomatic treatment + Anti-microbial therapy.

Recommended Antibiotics (Adults)

Antibiotic Dosing Duration* Remarks
Phenoxymethylpenicillin (Pen V) 500 mg PO q6h or 1 g PO q12h 5–10 days Preferred
Amoxicillin 500 mg PO q8h 5–10 days Preferred
Erythromycin Ethylsuccinate 800 mg PO q12h 5–10 days Alternative

*Consider 10 days in high-prevalence settings of rheumatic fever or in patients with a history of rheumatic fever/rheumatic heart disease who are aged 3–21 years.

Recommended Antibiotics (Children)

Antibiotic Dosing Duration Remarks
Phenoxymethylpenicillin (Pen V) 25–50 mg/kg/day PO q6h (max 1 g/day) 10 days Preferred
Amoxicillin 50 mg/kg/day PO q12–24h (max 1 g/day) 10 days Preferred
Cephalexin 25–50 mg/kg/day PO q12h (max 3 g/day) 10 days Alternative
Erythromycin Ethylsuccinate 40–50 mg/kg/day PO q12h (max 4 g/day) 14 days Alternative
Reference: National Antimicrobial Guideline, 4th Edition (2024) — Clinical Pathways for Primary Care: Acute Pharyngitis.
Any calculations must be rechecked and should not be used alone to guide patient care.
