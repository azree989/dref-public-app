Warfarin Dose Initiation Pathway

Criteria for “Sensitive” patients (tend to need lower initial doses):
  • Elderly or frail; debilitated, malnourished, or low body weight
  • Congestive heart failure or liver disease
  • On interacting medicines that increase sensitivity (e.g., amiodarone)
  • Other conditions known to enhance sensitivity to warfarin
Use lower starting doses and monitor more closely.
