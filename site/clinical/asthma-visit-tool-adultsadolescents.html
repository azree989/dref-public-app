Section: Patient Counselling & Education

Key topics to discuss and document:

  • Managing identified risk factors (e.g., smoking, allergens)
  • Adherence to treatment and correct inhaler technique
  • Non-pharmacological strategies (e.g., regular physical activity)
  • Providing a written Asthma Action Plan (Green-Yellow-Red zones)
  • Ensuring follow-up frequency and ongoing monitoring
