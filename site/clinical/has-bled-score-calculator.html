HAS-BLED Score Calculator

Inputs

Dialysis, kidney transplant, or serum creatinine ≥ 200 µmol/L (~2.3 mg/dL).
Cirrhosis, or bilirubin > 2× ULN with AST/ALT/ALP > 3× ULN.
Time in therapeutic range (TTR) < 60%.
Concomitant antiplatelets or NSAIDs (consider gastroprotection if needed).
≥ 8 drinks/week or excess intake.

Result

Interpretation (guide):
  • 0–2: Lower bleeding risk.
  • ≥3: Higher bleeding risk — mitigate modifiable factors and follow up more closely.
